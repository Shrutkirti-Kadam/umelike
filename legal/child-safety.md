# Child Safety Standards

**umelike** · Last updated 28 September 2026

umelike is a dating app for adults. This page sets out our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), how we enforce them, and how to reach the person responsible for them. It is published to satisfy Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and, more importantly, because it is true.

## 1. Adults only

- You must be 18 or older to create an umelike account. Sign-up asks your age in the app and your date of birth on the website; anyone under 18 is refused, and a date of birth is stored privately, never shown.
- Age is a hard rule, not a preference. A profile that is found to belong to a minor is removed and the account is permanently banned.
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

## 2. What is never allowed

The following are prohibited on umelike, in profiles, photos, prompts and messages, without exception:

- Any sexual content involving a minor, real or depicted, including drawings, edits and AI-generated imagery. This is child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
- Sexualising a minor in any way: comments, requests, role-play, or "ageplay" that presents a participant as a child.
- Grooming behaviour: attempting to build trust with a minor, asking a minor for photos or contact details, or encouraging a minor to move to another app.
- Soliciting, sharing, or trading CSAM, or linking to places where it can be found.
- Pretending to be a minor, or misrepresenting your age.
- Using umelike to contact or arrange to meet anyone under 18.

## 3. How we enforce this

**Reporting inside the app.** Every conversation has a ⋯ menu with **Report**. Every profile you see in Discover has a **Report or block** link. One of the reasons offered is **Underage**. Reports are confidential: the person reported is never told who reported them.

**Reporting from outside the app.** Anyone — a member or not — can write to **safety@umelike.in**. You do not need an account.

**Review.** A human reviews every report. Reports about a minor or about sexual content involving a minor are treated as the highest priority.

**Removal.** When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM, we remove it immediately and permanently ban the account. Bans are attached to the account's sign-in identity, so the same person cannot simply create a new account. We preserve the evidence as the law requires and delete it when the law allows.

**Reporting to authorities.** We report CSAM and attempts to exploit minors to the relevant authorities and cooperate with law enforcement. In India this includes the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in, helpline 1930). Where the law requires or permits, we also report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline.

**Message filtering.** Messages pass through a server-side term filter before delivery. Terms associated with the exploitation of minors are on that list and a message containing one is not delivered.

## 4. What we do not do

- We do not use members' photos or messages to train any model, and we do not scan private messages for anything other than the term filter described above.
- We do not accept payment to restore an account banned under this policy.
- We do not tell a reported person who reported them, ever.

## 5. Compliance with law

umelike complies with the child protection laws that apply to it, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules in India, and with Google Play's Developer Program Policies, including the Child Safety Standards policy. If a law requires more of us than this page says, the law wins.

Call **112** (India) or your local emergency number. In India, **Childline 1098** is a free 24-hour helpline for children in need of care and protection.
